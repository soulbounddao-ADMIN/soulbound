# Persona Clip Recorder Manual QA

- Record a short clip, stop, and confirm completion appears only after the upload finishes.
- Deny camera permission and confirm "continue" advances without a clip.
- Choose "skip" before recording and confirm no clip is attached.
- Throttle the signed upload and confirm the UI stays disabled without firing completion twice.
- Confirm there is no post-record playback, retake, file picker, or editing control.
